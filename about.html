About Quiz Master 🇷🇼

Welcome to Quiz Master, an educational quiz platform created to make learning, practising and exploring knowledge simple, accessible and enjoyable.

Quiz Master gives people an opportunity to test what they know, discover information they may not know yet and enjoy learning through interactive questions. It supports English and Kinyarwanda to make the learning experience accessible to more people.

Our Purpose

I made Quiz Master so that people can use it as a helpful place to learn about things they do not know, remind themselves of what they already know, understand their knowledge better, explore questions they have and relax their minds through enjoyable learning activities.

The goal is not only to answer questions or earn points. It is also to encourage curiosity, build understanding, refresh memory and make learning a positive part of everyday life.

What You Can Learn

Quiz Master currently focuses on two main categories:

  • Road Safety: Learn about road signs, traffic rules, pedestrian safety and responsible road use.
  • General Knowledge: Explore topics such as geography, history, science, technology, Rwanda, Africa and other areas of general knowledge.

Available Languages

Quiz Master supports two languages:

  • English
  • Kinyarwanda

Users can choose their preferred language when setting up a quiz, helping them practise and learn more comfortably.

How Quiz Master Works

  1. Open the Quiz Master homepage.
  2. Choose your preferred language.
  3. Select a quiz category.
  4. Start answering the questions.
  5. Submit your answers and read the explanations.
  6. Review your results and practise again.

Learning Through Practice

Quiz Master uses questions, answers, explanations and results to make learning interactive. A quiz result can help you see which questions you answered correctly and which topics may need more attention.

You do not need to know every answer before you begin. The experience is intended to encourage practice, curiosity and continuous learning.

Our Commitment

We aim to keep Quiz Master simple, useful and accessible. The platform may receive updates, new questions and improvements over time.

Educational note: Quiz Master is designed for learning and practice. Road safety questions are educational and should not replace official traffic laws, professional instruction or guidance from relevant authorities.

Access and Availability

Quiz Master is available through its website. Some features may require an internet connection, and availability of offline content depends on what has already been saved on your device.
