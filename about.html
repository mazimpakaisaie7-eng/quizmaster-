About Quiz Master 🇷🇼

Welcome to Quiz Master Rwanda, a bilingual educational quiz platform designed to make learning, practising and exploring knowledge simple and enjoyable.

Quiz Master helps users test their knowledge, discover new information and practise through interactive questions, multiple-choice answers, quiz results and educational explanations. The platform supports English and Kinyarwanda.

Our Purpose

Quiz Master aims to provide a helpful learning space where users can explore subjects, refresh what they already know and improve their understanding through educational quiz activities.

Learning is not only about selecting the correct answer. It is also about understanding why an answer is correct, discovering new ideas and developing curiosity.

Quiz Categories

Quiz Master focuses on two main learning categories:

  • Road Safety: Learn about road signs, traffic lights, road markings, pedestrians, responsible road use and general traffic safety principles.
  • General Knowledge: Explore topics such as Rwanda, Africa, geography, history, science, technology and other general knowledge subjects.

Available Languages

Quiz Master supports English and Kinyarwanda. Users can select their preferred language when setting up a quiz. Questions, answer choices and explanations are provided in the selected language where translations are available.

Quiz Rounds, Timing and Scoring

A standard quiz round contains ten questions. The application uses different answer time limits for its two main categories:

  • Road Safety: The standard question time limit is 60 seconds.
  • General Knowledge: The standard question time limit is 30 seconds.

Correct answers are worth ten points each. The standard passing score is 70 percent. Results help users see how they performed during a quiz round.

How to Use Quiz Master

  1. Open the Quiz Master homepage.
  2. Choose your preferred language.
  3. Select Road Safety or General Knowledge.
  4. Confirm your selections.
  5. Start a quiz round.
  6. Read each question and its answer choices carefully.
  7. Select an answer within the available time.
  8. Review your score and available explanations.
  9. Continue practising with available quiz rounds.

Quiz Results and Learning Progress

Quiz Master provides an interactive way to practise knowledge using questions, answer choices, scores and explanations.

Available progress features may help users keep track of completed rounds, review answers, continue an unfinished session and follow their practice history on the device.

Quiz results are intended to support personal learning. They are not formal academic qualifications, professional certificates or official examination results.
