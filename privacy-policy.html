Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Quiz Master 🇷🇼 ("Quiz Master", "we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy of visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you visit or use our website.

Quiz Master is an educational quiz website offering general knowledge and road-safety learning activities. This policy applies to the website and its available features.

Important: Basic quiz gameplay does not require account registration. Your scores, progress, and preferences may be stored locally in your browser. Third-party services may separately process technical, analytics, or advertising information when those services are enabled.

1. Information We May Process

You can generally use the basic quiz without entering personal information such as your name, phone number, or home address.

Depending on your use of the website and the services enabled, the following information may be processed:

  • Quiz scores and gameplay progress.
  • Current, completed, and unlocked rounds.
  • Answers and question-review information.
  • Language, category, and game preferences.
  • Browser and device technical information.
  • Website usage and performance information.
  • Advertising-related information, where applicable.

2. Information You Provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include in your message. This may include your email address, message content, questions, suggestions, or technical reports.

We may use this information to respond to your message, review feedback, and address reported problems.

Please do not send passwords, private credentials, or unnecessary confidential information in ordinary support messages.

3. Quiz Gameplay and Local Storage

Quiz Master may use browser local storage and similar technologies to save information on your device.

This may include:

  • Quiz progress and current round.
  • Scores and best scores.
  • Completed and unlocked rounds.
  • Questions saved for review.
  • Language and category preferences.
  • Music and other game settings.

Information saved in local storage generally remains in your browser on your device. It is not automatically an online account or a copy stored on our server.

Clearing your browser's website data or local storage may delete saved scores, progress, preferences, and other locally stored information.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website and its third-party service providers may use cookies, local storage, pixels, web beacons, or similar technologies where applicable.

These technologies may be used to:

  • Remember website preferences.
  • Support website functionality.
  • Understand how visitors use the website.
  • Measure website performance.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Help detect invalid activity and misuse.

Some cookies or similar technologies may be set by third-party providers rather than directly by Quiz Master.

5. Google AdSense and Advertising

Quiz Master may use Google AdSense to display advertisements when advertising services are enabled.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize advertisements, subject to applicable laws, consent requirements, and available privacy settings.

Depending on the advertising configuration, information processed may include browser or device information, IP address, cookie identifiers, approximate location information, and interactions with advertisements.

Advertising and personalization options may vary according to your location and privacy choices.

For more information, visit:

  • How Google uses information for advertising
  • Google My Ad Center

6. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Depending on your country or region, applicable laws may require consent or other privacy choices before certain advertising cookies or technologies are used.

Where required, advertising services should be configured with an appropriate consent mechanism and should respect the choices made by visitors.

Available privacy choices may differ according to your location and the services being used.

If a consent message or privacy control is displayed, review the available options before making your choice.

7. Google Analytics

If Google Analytics is enabled on the website, it may be used to understand how visitors interact with Quiz Master and to improve website content and functionality.

Depending on its configuration, analytics may process:

  • Pages visited.
  • Browser and device information.
  • General website interactions.
  • Approximate geographic information.
  • Technical information about website activity.

Google Analytics is operated by Google and is subject to Google's applicable privacy terms and practices.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy .

8. How Information May Be Used

Information processed through the website or its service providers may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the website.
  • Save gameplay progress and preferences locally.
  • Improve quiz questions and explanations.
  • Understand website usage and performance.
  • Respond to messages and feedback.
  • Maintain website security.
  • Detect invalid activity and misuse.
  • Display and measure advertisements, where enabled.
  • Meet applicable legal requirements.

9. Third-Party Services

Quiz Master may rely on third-party providers to host, operate, analyze, or support the website.

Depending on the services actually enabled, these may include:

  • Google AdSense for advertising.
  • Google Analytics for website analytics.
  • Vercel for website hosting and delivery.
  • Other technical infrastructure providers, if used.

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies, terms, and applicable laws. Their practices are not entirely controlled by Quiz Master.

10. Data Sharing

Quiz Master does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate the website, provide requested services, measure usage, or display advertisements.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable laws, respond to valid legal requests, protect website security, investigate misuse, or protect the rights and safety of users and others.

11. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable measures to protect the website and information processed through it.

However, no website, internet connection, browser, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data Retention

Gameplay information saved locally may remain on your device until it is deleted, overwritten, or removed when browser website data is cleared.

Emails sent to us may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to messages, maintain relevant records, or meet applicable legal requirements.

Third-party providers determine their own data retention practices under their applicable policies and settings.

13. Children's Privacy

Quiz Master provides educational quiz and learning activities. Basic gameplay does not require users to provide personal details such as a name, phone number, or email address.

We do not intentionally ask children to provide unnecessary personal information to use the basic quiz.

Third-party advertising and analytics services may have their own age-related requirements and restrictions. These services must be configured in accordance with applicable laws and platform policies, including requirements concerning children and personalized advertising.

14. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information processed about you.

These rights may include:

  • Requesting information about data processing.
  • Requesting access to certain personal information.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.

Clearing local browser data does not necessarily delete information independently held by third-party providers.
