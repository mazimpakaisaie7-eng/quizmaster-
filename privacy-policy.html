These third parties may use cookies or similar technologies to display, measure, and personalize advertisements. Google may use information from your visits to this and other websites to provide relevant advertising.

3. Cookies

Advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies when displaying advertisements.

4. Data Sharing

Quiz Master does not sell or intentionally collect personal information such as your name, phone number, or email address for advertising purposes.

Third-party advertising providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Quiz Master is a general knowledge game and does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

6. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Any changes will be posted on this page.

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Quiz Master developer.
