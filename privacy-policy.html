Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Quiz Master Rwanda ("Quiz Master", "we", "us" or "our") handles information when you visit or use our website.

Quiz Master Rwanda is an educational platform that provides interactive quizzes in Road Safety and General Knowledge, in English and Kinyarwanda. It also provides a Learning Centre with educational articles.

Privacy summary: Basic quiz gameplay does not require account registration. The application can save quiz progress, scores, language preferences and other game settings in your browser. Google Analytics, Google AdSense and website hosting services may process technical or usage information when their services are active.

1. Information We May Process

Basic use of Quiz Master does not require you to provide your name, phone number or home address.

Depending on the features you use, your browser settings and the third-party services active on the website, information may include:

  • Quiz scores and gameplay progress.
  • Current and completed quiz rounds.
  • Answers and questions saved for review.
  • Selected language and quiz category.
  • Music and other application preferences.
  • Browser type and device information.
  • Pages visited and website interactions.
  • Technical information associated with website requests.
  • Advertising and analytics information where those services are active.

The information processed depends on how you use the website and how your browser and third-party services are configured.

2. Information You Choose to Provide

If you contact Quiz Master by email, we receive the information you choose to include in your message. This may include your email address, message content, questions, suggestions or technical reports.

We use this information to review your message, respond when possible and address reported issues.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, account credentials or unnecessary confidential information in ordinary support messages.

3. Quiz Progress and Browser Storage

Quiz Master uses browser storage, including local storage where supported, to remember certain gameplay information and preferences.

Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Quiz progress and current session information.
  • Scores and best-score information.
  • Completed rounds and progress information.
  • Answers and questions saved for review.
  • Information used to manage previously used questions.
  • Selected language and quiz category.
  • Music and other game settings.

Information saved in local storage is generally stored in your browser on your device. Saving gameplay information locally does not, by itself, mean that an online account has been created or that the information has been uploaded to our website server.

Clearing this website's browser data or local storage may remove saved progress, scores, preferences and review information.

4. Cookies, Cache and Similar Technologies

Quiz Master uses browser technologies to support website operation. These may include local storage, browser cache and service-worker caching.

These technologies can help the website remember settings, load website resources and support available offline features.

Third-party services, where enabled, may also use cookies, pixels, web beacons or similar technologies for analytics, advertising, measurement or security.

You can manage or clear cookies and website data through your browser settings. Blocking or clearing certain storage technologies may affect saved progress or some website features.

5. Google Analytics

Quiz Master uses Google Analytics to help understand how visitors use the website and to improve website content, usability and performance.

Depending on its configuration and your browser or privacy settings, Google Analytics may process information such as:

  • Pages visited and website interactions.
  • Browser and device information.
  • Approximate geographic information.
  • Technical information about website activity.
  • Cookies or similar identifiers used for measurement.

Google processes information under its own applicable privacy policies and service terms.

Read: Google Privacy Policy .

More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

6. Google AdSense and Advertising

Quiz Master is configured to display Google AdSense advertising on the homepage only. Advertisements are not intentionally placed in the Learning Centre or on this Privacy Policy page.

When AdSense advertisements are active and served, Google and its advertising partners may process information to deliver, measure and support advertising.

Depending on applicable settings, laws and privacy choices, advertising may be personalized or non-personalized.

Advertising technologies may include cookies, browser identifiers, device information and other similar technologies.

The information and technologies used depend on Google's configuration, the visitor's location, applicable requirements and available privacy choices.

For more information, visit:

  • How Google uses information for advertising
  • Google My Ad Center
  • Google AdSense Help

7. Privacy Choices and Advertising Consent

Privacy and consent requirements can differ depending on the country or region from which you visit the website.

Where applicable laws or third-party service policies require consent for cookies, identifiers or certain advertising activities, the relevant services must be configured to respect those requirements and visitor choices.

This Privacy Policy explains information practices. It does not itself collect consent or replace a consent-management tool where one is required.

If a consent message or privacy control is displayed, review the available choices before making your selection.

8. Website Hosting and Technical Services

Quiz Master is hosted using Vercel. When delivering and protecting the website, the hosting provider may process technical information associated with website requests. This may include IP addresses, browser information and server or security logs.

Vercel processes information under its own applicable privacy policy and service terms.

Read: Vercel Privacy Policy .

9. How Information May Be Used

Information processed through Quiz Master and its service providers may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the website.
  • Save gameplay progress and preferences on your device.
  • Improve quiz questions and learning materials.
  • Understand website usage and performance.
  • Respond to questions, feedback and correction requests.
  • Maintain website security and reliability.
  • Detect invalid activity and misuse.
  • Display and measure advertising where enabled.
  • Meet applicable legal and service requirements.

10. Third-Party Services

Depending on the services active on the website, Quiz Master may rely on third parties such as:

  • Google Analytics for website measurement.
  • Google AdSense for homepage advertising.
  • Vercel for website hosting and delivery.

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies, service terms and applicable laws. Their processing practices are not entirely controlled by Quiz Master.

11. Information Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to host the website, provide analytics, deliver advertising, maintain security or operate other enabled website features.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to respond to valid legal requests, investigate misuse, protect website security or protect the rights and safety of users and others.

12. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable measures to protect the website and information processed through it.

However, no website, internet connection, browser or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Data Retention

Gameplay information saved locally may remain on your device until it is deleted, overwritten or removed when browser website data is cleared.

Emails sent to us may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to messages, review relevant issues or meet applicable legal requirements.

Third-party providers manage their own data retention practices under their applicable policies and service settings.

14. Children's Privacy

Quiz Master provides educational quiz and learning activities. Basic gameplay does not require account registration.

We aim to avoid requesting unnecessary personal information through the basic quiz features.

Third-party analytics and advertising services may have separate age-related requirements and restrictions. Where applicable, those services must be configured in accordance with relevant laws and platform policies.

Parents or guardians who have a privacy concern about information submitted through a contact message may contact us using the email below.

15. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information processed about you.

These rights may include:

  • Requesting information about data processing.
  • Requesting access to certain personal information.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion where legally applicable.
  • Objecting to or restricting certain processing.
  • Managing available cookie and advertising choices.

These rights may be subject to legal conditions and limitations. You can contact us with a privacy question or request.

16. Deleting Locally Stored Data

You can generally remove locally saved Quiz Master information by clearing this website's data or local storage through your browser settings.

This may remove:

  • Saved quiz scores and progress.
  • Completed rounds and review information.
  • Language and category preferences.
  • Music and other game settings.

Clearing browser data does not necessarily delete information independently held by Google, Vercel or other third-party providers.

17. International Data Processing

Some third-party providers used by the website may process information in countries other than the country where a visitor is located.

Such processing is subject to the applicable requirements and safeguards used by the relevant providers.

18. External Website Links

Quiz Master may contain links to external websites and services, including Google and Vercel.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of independently operated third-party websites.

Please review the privacy policies of external websites before providing them with personal information.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, its features, third-party services or applicable requirements change.

Updated information will be published on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be changed when the policy is revised.
