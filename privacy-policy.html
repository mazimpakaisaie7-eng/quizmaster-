Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Quiz Master 🇷🇼 ("Quiz Master", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you visit or use the Quiz Master website and its available features.

This policy describes the website's current basic quiz experience and the third-party services that may be used to operate, measure, and support the website.

Important: Basic quiz gameplay is designed not to require account registration. Quiz scores, settings, and progress may be saved in your browser or on your device. Third-party services such as Google Analytics and Google AdSense may process information when enabled on the website.

1. Information We May Process

You can generally play the basic quiz without creating an account or entering personal information such as your name, phone number, or home address.

Depending on how you use the website and which services are enabled, the following types of information may be processed:

  • Quiz scores and gameplay progress.
  • Current, completed, and unlocked rounds.
  • Quiz answers and review information.
  • Game settings and language preferences.
  • Music and other user preferences.
  • Browser, device, and technical information.
  • Website usage and analytics information.
  • Advertising and advertisement interaction information.

2. Information You Send to Us

If you contact us by email, we may receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address, message content, questions, suggestions, or information about a technical problem.

We use this information to understand and respond to your message, review feedback, correct reported problems, and improve the website where appropriate.

Please do not send passwords, private credentials, payment information, or other confidential information in ordinary support messages.

3. Quiz Gameplay and Local Storage

Quiz Master may use browser local storage and similar technologies to save information on your device.

This information may include:

  • Your quiz progress.
  • Your current round.
  • Your scores and best score.
  • Completed and unlocked rounds.
  • Questions saved for review.
  • Language and category preferences.
  • Music and other game settings.

Information saved in local storage is generally kept in your browser on your device. It is not automatically the same as information stored in an online user account.

Clearing your browser's website data or local storage may remove saved scores, progress, settings, and other locally stored information.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Quiz Master and third-party services used by the website may use cookies, local storage, pixels, web beacons, or similar technologies.

These technologies may be used to:

  • Remember preferences and settings.
  • Support website functionality.
  • Understand how visitors use the website.
  • Measure website performance.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Help detect invalid activity and protect services.

Third-party technologies may be controlled by the relevant service providers and may be subject to their own privacy policies.

5. Google AdSense and Advertising

Quiz Master may use Google AdSense to display advertisements on the website.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize advertisements, depending on applicable laws, available consent choices, and advertising settings.

Advertising-related information may include browser or device information, IP address, cookie identifiers, approximate location information, and interactions with advertisements.

The type of advertisements displayed may depend on factors such as your location, privacy choices, advertising settings, and the services available to the website.

You can learn more about Google's advertising and privacy practices here:

  • How Google uses information for advertising
  • Google My Ad Center

6. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Depending on your country or region, applicable laws may require consent or other privacy choices before certain advertising cookies or technologies are used.

Where required, the website should use an appropriate consent mechanism and respect the choices made by visitors.

Available choices may differ depending on your location and the services being used.

If a consent message or privacy control is presented, you should review the choices and select the option that reflects your preferences.

7. Google Analytics

Quiz Master may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the website and to help improve its content and functionality.

Depending on the configuration, analytics may process:

  • Pages visited.
  • Browser and device information.
  • General website interactions.
  • Approximate geographic information.
  • Technical information about website activity.

Google Analytics processes information under Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy .

8. How Information May Be Used

Information processed through Quiz Master or its service providers may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the website.
  • Save gameplay progress and preferences locally.
  • Improve quiz questions and explanations.
  • Understand website usage and performance.
  • Respond to messages and feedback.
  • Maintain website security.
  • Detect invalid activity and misuse.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Meet applicable legal requirements.

9. Third-Party Services

Quiz Master may rely on third-party providers to host, operate, analyze, or support the website.

Depending on the services currently enabled, these may include:

  • Google AdSense for advertising.
  • Google Analytics for website analytics.
  • Vercel for website hosting and delivery.
  • Other technical infrastructure providers, if used.

10. Data Sharing

Quiz Master does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate the website, provide requested services, measure usage, or display advertisements.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable laws, respond to valid legal requests, protect website security, investigate misuse, or protect the rights and safety of users and others.

11. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable measures to protect the website and information processed through it.

However, no website, internet connection, browser, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should also keep your device, browser, and personal accounts secure.

12. Data Retention

Gameplay information saved locally may remain on your device until it is deleted, overwritten, or removed when browser website data is cleared.

Emails sent to us may remain in our email service for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the message, maintain relevant records, or meet applicable legal requirements.

Third-party providers determine their own retention practices under their applicable policies and settings.

13. Children's Privacy

Quiz Master is an educational quiz website intended to provide general learning and practice activities.

Basic gameplay does not require users to provide personal details such as a name, phone number, or email address.

We do not intentionally ask children to provide unnecessary personal information to play the basic quiz.

Advertising and analytics services may have their own age-related requirements and restrictions. Such services should be configured and used in accordance with applicable laws and platform policies.

These rights may include:

  • Requesting access to certain personal information.
