Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Quiz Master 🇷🇼 ("Quiz Master", "we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy of visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you visit or use our website.

Quiz Master is an educational quiz website that provides interactive learning activities in English and Kinyarwanda. Its content includes road safety and general knowledge quizzes, learning materials, and related website features.

Important: Basic quiz gameplay does not require account registration. Quiz progress, scores, answers saved for review, and preferences may be stored locally in your browser. Google Analytics, Google AdSense, the hosting provider, and other services may separately process technical or usage information when their features are active.

1. Information We May Process

You can generally use the basic quiz without entering personal details such as your name, phone number, or home address.

Depending on how you use the website and which services are active, information processed may include:

  • Quiz scores and gameplay progress.
  • Current, completed, and unlocked quiz rounds.
  • Answers and question-review information saved by the app.
  • Selected language and quiz category.
  • Music and other game preferences.
  • Browser, device, and technical information.
  • Website visits, interactions, and performance information.
  • Advertising-related information where advertisements are served.

The information processed depends on the features you use, your browser settings, and the configuration of third-party services.

2. Information You Choose to Provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include in your message. This may include your email address, message content, questions, suggestions, or technical reports.

We may use this information to respond to your message, review feedback, and address reported problems.

Please do not send passwords, private credentials, or unnecessary confidential information in ordinary support messages.

3. Quiz Gameplay and Browser Storage

Quiz Master uses browser storage technologies, such as local storage, to support gameplay and remember certain settings on your device.

Depending on the features you use, locally stored information may include:

  • Quiz progress and current session information.
  • Scores and best scores.
  • Completed and unlocked rounds.
  • Questions and answers saved for review.
  • Used-question information to help manage question repetition.
  • Language and category preferences.
  • Music and other game settings.

Information saved in browser local storage generally remains on your device. Saving information in local storage does not, by itself, create an online account or mean that the information has been uploaded to our server.

Clearing your browser's website data or local storage may delete saved scores, progress, preferences, and other locally stored information.

4. Cookies, Cache, and Similar Technologies

Quiz Master and its third-party service providers may use cookies, local storage, browser cache, pixels, web beacons, or similar technologies where applicable.

These technologies may be used to:

  • Remember website and gameplay preferences.
  • Support website functionality.
  • Store app resources for loading and offline features.
  • Understand how visitors use the website.
  • Measure website performance.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Help protect services and detect invalid activity.

Some cookies and similar technologies may be set by third-party providers rather than directly by Quiz Master.

You can manage or delete cookies and website data through your browser settings. Blocking certain storage technologies may affect some website features or saved gameplay progress.

5. Google Analytics

Quiz Master uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the website and to help improve its content, usability, and performance.

Depending on its configuration, Google Analytics may process information such as:

  • Pages visited and general website interactions.
  • Browser and device information.
  • Approximate geographic information.
  • Technical information about website activity.
  • Cookies or similar identifiers used for measurement.

Google Analytics is provided by Google. Google processes information according to its applicable privacy policies and service terms.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy .

You can also read: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

6. Google AdSense and Advertising

Quiz Master uses Google AdSense for advertising when advertisements are enabled and available on the website.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, browser information, device information, or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and support advertisements.

Depending on the advertising settings, applicable requirements, and your privacy choices, advertising may be personalized or non-personalized.

Personalized advertising may use information such as previous activity or interests to make ads more relevant. Non-personalized advertising may use contextual information, while certain cookies or identifiers may still be used for purposes such as frequency capping and aggregated reporting.

Advertising availability, personalization, and data processing may vary according to your location, Google's settings, and applicable privacy requirements.

For more information, visit:

  • How Google uses information for advertising
  • Google My Ad Center
  • Google AdSense Help

7. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Privacy and consent requirements may differ depending on the country or region from which you visit the website.

Where applicable laws or Google's policies require consent for advertising cookies, identifiers, or personalized advertising, the relevant advertising services must be configured to respect those requirements and the choices made by visitors.

A privacy policy explains data practices, but it does not replace a consent mechanism where one is legally or contractually required.

If a consent message or privacy control is displayed, please review the available choices before making your selection.

8. Website Hosting and Technical Services

Quiz Master is hosted using Vercel. As part of delivering and protecting the website, the hosting provider may process technical information associated with website requests, such as IP addresses, browser information, device information, and server or security logs.

The hosting provider processes information under its own applicable privacy practices and terms.

Learn more: Vercel Privacy Policy .

9. How Information May Be Used

Information processed through the website and its service providers may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the website.
  • Save gameplay progress and preferences on your device.
  • Improve quiz questions and learning materials.
  • Understand website usage and performance.
  • Respond to messages and feedback.
  • Maintain website security and reliability.
  • Detect invalid activity and misuse.
  • Display and measure advertisements where enabled.
  • Meet applicable legal and policy requirements.

10. Third-Party Services

Quiz Master relies on third-party services to support website hosting, analytics, and advertising. Depending on the features being used, these include:

  • Google Analytics for website measurement and analytics.
  • Google AdSense for advertising.
  • Vercel for website hosting and delivery.

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies, service terms, and applicable laws. Their data practices are not entirely controlled by Quiz Master.

11. Data Sharing

Quiz Master does not sell personal information collected directly through the website.

Information may be processed by the service providers described in this policy where necessary to host the website, provide analytics, deliver advertisements, maintain security, or provide other website features.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable laws, respond to valid legal requests, protect website security, investigate misuse, or protect the rights and safety of users and others.

12. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable measures to protect the website and information processed through it.

However, no website, internet connection, browser, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Data Retention

Gameplay information saved locally may remain on your device until it is deleted, overwritten, or removed when browser website data is cleared.

Emails sent to us may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to messages, maintain relevant records, or meet applicable legal requirements.

Google Analytics, Google AdSense, and hosting providers manage their own data retention practices under their applicable policies and settings.

14. Children's Privacy

Quiz Master provides educational quiz and learning activities. Basic gameplay does not require users to register an account or provide personal details such as a name, phone number, or email address.

We do not intentionally request unnecessary personal information from children through the basic quiz features.

Third-party analytics and advertising services have their own age-related requirements and restrictions. Where applicable, these services must be configured in accordance with relevant laws and platform policies, including requirements concerning child-directed content and advertising.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information through a contact message, please contact us so that the concern can be reviewed.

15. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information processed about you.

These rights may include:

  • Requesting information about data processing.
  • Requesting access to certain personal information.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion where legally applicable.
  • Objecting to or restricting certain processing.
  • Managing available advertising and cookie choices.

These rights may be subject to legal conditions and limitations. You can contact us using the email address provided below to submit a privacy question or request.

Clearing local browser data does not necessarily delete information independently held by Google, the hosting provider, or other third-party services.
