About Quiz Master

Quiz Master is an interactive knowledge game created to help users test their general knowledge in a simple and enjoyable way. The game presents questions from different subjects and gives players a score based on their correct answers.

Each quiz round contains a set of questions with a limited amount of time for each question. Players can use their results to see how well they performed and continue progressing through additional rounds.

The goal of Quiz Master is to make learning more engaging while encouraging curiosity about Rwanda, Africa and the wider world.

How Quiz Master Works

  1. Select Start Quiz to begin a new quiz.
  2. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct.
  3. Answer within the available time for each question.
  4. Earn points for correct answers and keep track of your score.
  5. Complete a round and check your final result.
  6. Reach the required score to unlock additional quiz rounds.

Scoring and Progress

Quiz Master uses a simple scoring system. Correct answers add points to your score, while incorrect answers do not add points.

Your completed rounds and quiz progress can be saved in your browser so that you can continue your progress when the Continue Quiz option is available.

  • Each round contains 10 questions.
  • Each question has a time limit.
  • Correct answers earn points.
  • A score of 60% or higher is used to unlock the next round.
  • Up to 10 quiz rounds are available in the current game structure.

Why Play Quiz Master?

Quiz games can be a simple way to review facts, discover new topics and challenge your memory. Quiz Master brings different knowledge categories together in one place so that users can move between subjects and track their progress.

Whether you enjoy Geography, History, Science, Technology, Rwanda, Africa or Sports, Quiz Master gives you an opportunity to test what you know and learn from questions you may not have encountered before.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Quiz Master?

Quiz Master is a free online knowledge quiz game with questions covering multiple categories.

How many questions are in a round?

The current game structure uses 10 questions per round.

How do I unlock another round?

A round can unlock the next round when the required score is reached. The current configuration uses 60% as the unlock requirement.

Can I continue a quiz later?

If quiz progress has been saved in your browser, the Continue Quiz option can be used to resume available progress.

Which subjects are available?

Quiz Master covers subjects such as Geography, History, Science, Technology, Rwanda, Africa, Sports and General Knowledge.
