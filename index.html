About Quiz Master

What Is Quiz Master?

Quiz Master 🇷🇼 is an educational quiz game that helps players test their knowledge and learn useful facts.

Learn While Playing

Read each question carefully, choose an answer and learn from the explanation when one is available.

How to Play

Choose your language and category, then answer ten questions per round. Each question has a 30-second timer.

Quiz Progress

Earn points and work through the available quiz rounds as you improve your results.

Road Traffic Rules

Practise road signs, road safety and traffic-rule knowledge with questions in your chosen language.

General Knowledge

Explore questions about geography, science, history, Rwanda, Africa, sports and other subjects.

Quiz Master Features

🎯 Score Points

Answer questions correctly to earn points.

🏆 Unlock Rounds

Progress through rounds according to your results.

📚 Learn From Questions

Use questions and explanations to practise knowledge.

📱 Mobile Friendly

Play on phones, tablets and larger screens.
