About Quiz Master

What Is Quiz Master?

Quiz Master 🇷🇼 is an educational knowledge quiz game designed to help players test their knowledge while discovering useful facts from different subjects and regions around the world.

Learning With Quiz Master

Each quiz question gives players an opportunity to recall information, learn from correct answers, and improve their knowledge through repeated practice.

How to Play

Start a quiz, read each question carefully, choose the answer you think is correct, and continue through the round. Your score depends on the answers you give.

Quiz Progress

Quiz Master organizes gameplay into rounds. Completing rounds allows players to track their progress and work toward unlocking additional challenges.

Why Play Quiz Master?

Quiz games can make learning interactive and enjoyable. Quiz Master brings together questions from several knowledge areas so players can challenge themselves across different subjects.

Built for Knowledge

The goal of Quiz Master is to provide a simple, accessible and enjoyable way to practice general knowledge and discover information about Rwanda, Africa and the wider world.

Quiz Master Features

🎯 Score Points

Answer questions correctly to earn points and measure your performance during each quiz round.

🏆 Unlock Rounds

Quiz Master is designed not only for competition, but also as an interactive way to review facts and learn new information.
