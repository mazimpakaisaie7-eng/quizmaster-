How to Use Quiz Master

1. Choose your quiz

Select your language and choose Road Safety or General Knowledge, then confirm your choices to begin.

2. Answer questions

Read each question, select an answer, and submit it before the timer ends. The timer depends on the quiz category.

3. Review your results

At the end, check your score, correct answers, incorrect answers, and the answers you selected.

4. Track your progress

Use the rounds and weekly scores sections to see saved progress when score-saving is supported by the game engine.

5. Settings and music

Open Settings to turn the background music on or off.
