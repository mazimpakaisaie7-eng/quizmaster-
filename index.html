About Quiz Master

What Is Quiz Master?

Quiz Master 🇷🇼 is an educational knowledge game designed to help players test their knowledge and discover useful facts from around the world.

Learning With Quiz Master

Each question gives players an opportunity to learn from correct answers and improve their knowledge through practice.

How to Play

Choose a language and quiz category, start a round, select an answer and submit it before the timer ends.

Quiz Progress

Complete quiz rounds, earn points and unlock additional challenges by meeting the required score.

Why Play Quiz Master?

Explore different subjects through interactive questions and discover interesting information.

Built for Knowledge

Quiz Master provides an accessible way to practice general knowledge and learn about Rwanda, Africa and the wider world.
