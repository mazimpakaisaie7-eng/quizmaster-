How to Play

  1. Press Start Quiz to begin.
  2. Each round contains 10 questions.
  3. You have 20 seconds to answer each question.
  4. A correct answer gives you 10 points.
  5. Score at least 60% to unlock the next round.
  6. Use Continue Quiz to resume saved progress.
