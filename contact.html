How to Send a Helpful Message

When reporting a problem, it may help to include:

  • The page or feature where the problem happened.
  • A short description of what went wrong.
  • The question number, if reporting a quiz question.
  • Your device and browser, if reporting a technical issue.
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the problem.

Please do not include passwords, payment details or other confidential information in your message.
