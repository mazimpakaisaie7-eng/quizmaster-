
# Quiz Master 🇷🇼 - Knowledge Quiz

Welcome to **Quiz Master Rwanda**, an educational quiz platform designed to make learning, practising and testing knowledge simple and enjoyable.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://quizmaster-liard-five.vercel.app/

---

## 📖 About Quiz Master

Quiz Master is an online educational quiz application that allows users to test their knowledge through interactive questions.

The platform supports English and Kinyarwanda and focuses on road safety and general knowledge.

Our goal is to provide an accessible learning experience that encourages curiosity, knowledge development and continuous learning.

## 🎯 Main Features

- English and Kinyarwanda language support.
- Road safety quiz questions.
- General knowledge quiz questions.
- Multiple-choice questions.
- Timed quiz sessions.
- Score tracking.
- Quiz results.
- Answer review.
- Multiple quiz rounds.
- Progress tracking.
- Responsive design for mobile phones and computers.
- Optional background music.
- Share functionality.
- Privacy and terms information pages.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Open the Quiz Master website.
2. Select your preferred language.
3. Choose a quiz category.
4. Choose whether to enable background music, if available.
5. Start the quiz.
6. Read each question carefully.
7. Select your answer and submit it.
8. Review your results after completing a round.

## 🏆 Scoring System

The quiz uses a points-based scoring system.

- Correct answers earn points.
- Results are displayed after completing a round.
- Progress can be tracked across quiz rounds.
- Some rounds may require a minimum score to unlock.

The scoring and round rules are controlled by the application's JavaScript configuration.
